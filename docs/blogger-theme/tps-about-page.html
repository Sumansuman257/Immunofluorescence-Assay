The Pipettes Solution

A lab-literacy project: calculators, Bench Notes, and decision-shaped teaching for molecular biology.

About

We exist so students and scientists can plan experiments with clear units, honest controls, and prose that respects both beginners and biosafety review.

What you will find

  • Lab tools — TCID50, cloning, ligation, cDNA, and related calculators.
  • Bench Notes — long-form teaching posts with steps-first structure, schematic figures, and citations.
  • Literacy over recipe dumps — we teach how to think; your signed institutional SOP remains authoritative.

How we write

Brand voice is practical and precise: navy and teal, expressive typography, figures that teach one idea each. We avoid flashy nonsense and we avoid pretending a blog post is a license to skip training.

Draft-first publishing

Many posts begin as unpublished drafts so citations, images, and safety language can be reviewed. If you are reading a live post, treat it as educational communication — not a substitute for local protocols.
