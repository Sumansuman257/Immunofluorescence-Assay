## Gap summary (current Pipettes)

- Welcome block is an inset gray card (weak brand, not full-bleed).
- Nav mixes science tools with exam/misc/entertainment links.
- No dedicated About page (`/p/about.html` 404).
- Tools exist but are not a first-class “Lab tools” section.
- Theme accent `#2e51a2` fights Bench Notes navy/teal system.
- Homepage feels blog-template; competitor feels curated portfolio + toolkit.

## Success bar

A visitor in 10 seconds knows: (1) brand, (2) what you get (calculators + Bench Notes), (3) where to click next — and the page feels more intentional than a personal academic portfolio site.
