⏱️ IFA Protocol click Start on any step

⏳ Timer ready 00:00
1 Fixation: 3.7% formaldehyde (10 min) OR 100% methanol (10 min) OR 80% methanol (20 min) at 4°C. 10-20 min
2 Wash: 2-3× with PBS. 100-200 µL per well (plate dependent). 5 min
3 Permeabilization: 0.2% Triton X-100 for 8-10 min. 10 min
4 Wash: 3× with PBS. 5 min
5 Blocking: 1% PBS-BSA, 30 min at RT. 30 min
6 Wash: 2-3× with PBS. 5 min
7 Primary Antibody: Diluted in 1% PBS-BSA. Incubate 1 hour at 37°C 60 min
8 Wash: 5-6× with PBS (100-200 µL/well). 10 min
9 Secondary Antibody: Anti-species IgG (1:1000) in 1% PBS-BSA. 30-60 min at RT/37°C. PROTECT FROM LIGHT! 60 min
10 Wash: 5-6× with PBS. Protect from light. 10 min
11 Optional DAPI: 1:2000 in PBS for nuclear staining. 5 min
12 Visualization: Examine under fluorescence microscope. Store in dark at 4°C in PBS. — no timer
⚠️ Critical: Protect fluorophore-conjugated antibodies from light! Wrap tubes in foil. Keep samples in dark during and after staining.
